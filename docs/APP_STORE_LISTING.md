## Privacy nutrition-label guidance

Use this as a review checklist. Confirm the final build and all integrated SDKs before you answer App Store Connect questions.

- The app does not collect data for the developer.
- The app does not track users across apps or websites.
- The app stores account tokens and optional Seerr credentials on the device in the Apple Keychain.
- The app stores offline media, artwork, download details, and playback position on the device.
- The app sends account and media activity to the Jellyfin and Seerr servers selected by the user. Those services are outside the developer's control.

- Seerr features are optional.
